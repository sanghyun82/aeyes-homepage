Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective: November 9, 2026 · 한국어

AEYES Co., Ltd. (주식회사 에이아이즈, the “Company”) publishes this Privacy Policy to protect users’ personal information under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and to handle related complaints promptly while providing the trial, license issuing and activation service (the “License Service”) of AEYES 3D Viewer (the “Software”).

The Software’s 3D model and drawing viewing features never send your files to the Company. This policy covers the information processed by the License Service. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

3. Destruction

Data past its retention period is deleted or anonymized by a job that runs every day. Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered, and backup copies are removed as the backup rotation proceeds.

4. Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide users’ personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

6. Your rights

  • You may at any time request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or withdraw your consent.
  • Send your request to the contact below; it is handled without delay after confirming your identity.
  • You can withdraw the optional phone consent by unchecking it in your next request or by contacting us; the stored phone number is then deleted.
  • When you ask for deletion, your contact data, notice records and installation data are deleted, and only the link between you and the computer history is removed. Per-PC trial records are kept for their retention period, so a deletion request does not restart the trial on the same PC.
  • If you hold a valid paid license, we confirm that you want to terminate it before deleting.

7. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS, TLS 1.2 or later)
  • Credentials are stored only as hashes; secrets such as signing keys are kept in separate access-restricted files
  • The administration site is reachable only from the company network, and administrators sign in with two-factor authentication (TOTP)
  • Administrator access records are kept and reviewed, and the database is backed up every day

8. Automatic collection

The Software collects no cookies, advertising identifiers or usage statistics. The license server records IP addresses and request paths for security and troubleshooting.
